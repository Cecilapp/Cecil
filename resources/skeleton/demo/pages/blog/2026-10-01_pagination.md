---
title: Pagination
description: The list of posts is split into several pages, according to the pagination configuration.
tags: [Cecil]
---
The blog section displays 2 posts per page: the following ones are displayed on the next pages (e.g.: `/blog/page/2/`).

The number of posts per page is defined globally in `cecil.yml` (`pages.pagination.max`), and can be changed for a section, in the front matter of its index page.
